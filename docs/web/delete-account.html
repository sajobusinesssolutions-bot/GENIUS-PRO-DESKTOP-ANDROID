Delete your Genius Pro POS account

Genius Pro POS is made by Saljoe Tech. You can delete your account and the data we keep for it at any time.

In the app — the quickest way

  1. Open Genius Pro POS, signed in as the business owner.
  2. Go to Menu and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete account, type your email address and tap Delete.

Without the app

Email us from the address you sign in with, and we will delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

Request deletion by email

What is deleted

  • Your account: email, name, password and sign-in sessions.
  • Your businesses and their cloud copies: sales, purchases, payments, stock, customers, suppliers and settings synced to our server.
  • Licences and the list of linked devices.

What is kept

  • Crash reports (what went wrong, app version, device model), with your account removed from them, so we can fix faults.
  • Books stored on your own phones stay there until you remove the app — we cannot reach them.
